Where the knowledge, data, and code behind this came from.

Source material

Every tasting note, pairing, and scale rating here started as my own study notes while reading Wine Folly: The Essential Guide to Wine by Madeline Puckette & Justin Hammack, and its companion site winefolly.com. The food categories on the Food page are grouped the same way Wine Folly's own pairing charts are — by what characteristic of the dish actually drives the match. This is a personal study tool: the wording and reasoning here are my own paraphrasing, not a reproduction of the book's text, charts, or infographics.

Built with

Python (Pydantic, PyYAML, Jinja2, openpyxl) turns the YAML notes into a SQLite database and a static site; the site itself is plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No server, no accounts, no tracking — everything ships as files and works in airplane mode once installed.
